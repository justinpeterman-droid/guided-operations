# Accuracy answer key - DRAFT for owner review

**Status: unverified.** Every answer below was written by reading the extracted
policy text, but none has been confirmed by the owner. Until it is, this file is
a proposal, not ground truth. Do not run an evaluation against it and report a
score as meaningful.

## How to review this

For each question, check three things:

1. **Is the question worth asking?** Would an officer really ask it? Strike any
   that would not come up in real work.
2. **Is the answer right?** The quote from the policy is included so you can
   check without opening the PDF.
3. **Is it the right citation?** Policy number and printed page.

Mark each row `KEEP`, `FIX` (with the correction), or `CUT`.

## What the categories mean

| Category  | Purpose                                                                                                                       |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| LOOKUP    | A single fact stated plainly in one policy. Most questions.                                                                   |
| PROCEDURE | A sequence or escalation the officer must get in the right order.                                                             |
| CROSS     | The answer sits in one policy but the officer might look in another.                                                          |
| REFUSE    | **The corpus cannot answer this.** The app must say so rather than invent. These are the most important questions in the set. |

A wrong answer on a REFUSE question is the worst possible result: it means the
application invents policy. Those are scored separately and the target is zero.

## Use of force and OC

### Q1 - LOOKUP

**Question:** Who can authorize me to be issued OC?

**Expected answer:** The Warden, Deputy/Assistant Warden, or Chief Security
Officer.

**Citation:** NCU 9.26.0, page 2

> "Authority for the issuance and use of OC by security personnel shall rest
> with the Warden, Deputy/Assistant Warden, or Chief Security Officer."

### Q2 - LOOKUP

**Question:** Do I have to warn an inmate before spraying OC in a planned use of
force?

**Expected answer:** Yes. Before using OC, staff must warn the inmate that OC
will be used unless they comply with orders.

**Citation:** NCU 9.26.0, page 2

> "Prior to the use of OC, staff will warn the inmate(s) that OC will be used
> unless the inmate complies with orders."

### Q3 - PROCEDURE

**Question:** What do I have to do before deploying OC in a planned use of
force?

**Expected answer:** Get authorization from the Duty Warden, and notify medical
and mental health staff. Then warn the inmate before spraying.

**Citation:** NCU 9.26.0, page 2

> "Prior to deploying OC during a planned use of force, staff will receive
> authorization from the Duty Warden and notify medical and mental health
> staff."

### Q4 - LOOKUP

**Question:** After an inmate is sprayed with OC, when do they get to
decontaminate?

**Expected answer:** As soon as it is tactically safe for the inmate and staff.
This applies to both planned and immediate uses of force.

**Citation:** NCU 9.26.0, pages 2 and 3

> "In all instances, inmates will be provided the opportunity to decontaminate
> with water as soon as it is tactically safe for the inmate and staff to do
> so."

### Q5 - LOOKUP

**Question:** Do I need photographs if an inmate is contaminated with OC?

**Expected answer:** Yes, for both planned and immediate uses of force.

**Citation:** NCU 9.26.0, pages 3

> "Photographs will be taken of any inmate(s) that has been contaminated with OC
> during an immediate use of force to document the incident appropriately."

### Q6 - LOOKUP

**Question:** How often is OC refresher training?

**Expected answer:** As needed, or every two years. Refresher training includes
a Level 3 contamination.

**Citation:** NCU 9.26.0, page 3

> "refresher training will be conducted as needed or every two years. Refresher
> training will include a level 3 contamination."

### Q7 - LOOKUP

**Question:** Can I be issued OC if I have not been sprayed in training?

**Expected answer:** No. Initial training requires a Level 1 exposure, and no
employee is issued OC without it.

**Citation:** NCU 9.26.0, page 3

> "All staff receiving initial training in OC shall have a Level one (1)
> exposure as part of this initial training. No employee shall be issued OC
> without this level 1 exposure."

### Q8 - LOOKUP

**Question:** What is a Level 2 OC exposure?

**Expected answer:** Indirect contact - for example, an officer contaminated
while subduing an inmate who was sprayed.

**Citation:** NCU 9.26.0, page 2

> "a level 2 exposure occurs when a subject has indirect contact with OC."

### Q9 - LOOKUP

**Question:** What do I do with my OC can when I transfer to another unit?

**Expected answer:** Return it to the appropriate authority. The same applies at
the end of employment.

**Citation:** NCU 9.26.0, page 2

> "Staff members must return their assigned can of OC to the appropriate
> authority at the end of their employment or upon transfer to another
> facility."

## Counts

### Q10 - PROCEDURE

**Question:** The count doesn't clear. What happens first?

**Expected answer:** The Shift Supervisor notifies the Shift Commander and
orders a recount.

**Citation:** NCU 9.05.0, page 2

> "If the count does not clear the first time, the shift supervisor will notify
> the Shift Commander and order a recount."

### Q11 - PROCEDURE

**Question:** The second count still doesn't clear. Then what?

**Expected answer:** The Shift Commander notifies the Chief Security Officer,
who orders all inmates working inside the perimeter fence back to their living
quarters for a third count - a Roster Count, where the officer check-marks each
inmate's name on the barracks bed roster.

**Citation:** NCU 9.05.0, page 2

> "the Shift Commander will notify the Chief Security Officer, who will order
> all inmates that work within the perimeter fence to return from their job site
> to their living quarters for another count (Roster Count)."

### Q12 - PROCEDURE

**Question:** Who has to be told if a count hasn't cleared after 30 minutes?

**Expected answer:** The Chief Security Officer, who in turn notifies the Warden
/ Deputy / Assistant Warden.

**Citation:** NCU 9.05.0, page 3

> "The Chief Security Officer will be notified at any time a count has not
> cleared within a thirty (30) minute time period. The Chief Security Officer
> will in turn notify the Warden/Deputy/Assistant Warden."

### Q13 - LOOKUP

**Question:** Who can declare an Emergency Count?

**Expected answer:** The Unit Warden or Duty Warden, after the fourth (Roster)
count fails to clear.

**Citation:** NCU 9.05.0, page 3

> "If the count is still not cleared after the fourth count (Roster Count), then
> an Emergency Count may be declared by the Unit Warden or Duty Warden."

### Q14 - LOOKUP

**Question:** Is a shape under a blanket an acceptable count?

**Expected answer:** No. Officers must confirm living, breathing flesh.
Flashlights may be used on night counts.

**Citation:** NCU 9.05.0, page 3

> "Officers conducting counts are required to ensure the inmate being counted is
> 'living and breathing flesh.' Flashlights may be used during night counts."

### Q15 - LOOKUP

**Question:** How often do I have to verify a trustee (class I-B) working
outside the perimeter?

**Expected answer:** Visually observe and verify each assigned trustee at a
minimum of once every hour.

**Citation:** NCU 9.05.0, page 4

> "Each supervisor assigned/responsible for trustee inmates will visually
> observe/verify the presence of each trustee assigned to their supervision at a
> minimum of once every hour."

### Q16 - LOOKUP

**Question:** Can an inmate help hand out count slips?

**Expected answer:** No. Inmate participation in any part of count activity is
prohibited, including preparation, processing and delivery of count slips.

**Citation:** NCU 9.05.0, page 5

> "Inmate participation in any portion of count activity is prohibited,
> including preparation, processing, or delivery of count slips."

### Q17 - LOOKUP

**Question:** Can a delivery truck leave the compound during count?

**Expected answer:** No. Vehicles do not enter or exit during counting, and
vehicles that cannot be easily searched are locked until the count clears.

**Citation:** NCU 9.05.0, page 4

> "Vehicles will not enter or exit the compound during counting."

### Q18 - LOOKUP

**Question:** What is an Out Count?

**Expected answer:** Inmates outside the unit or barracks on assignment -
outside work crews, gate passes, furloughs, court, hospital and other temporary
absences.

**Citation:** NCU 9.05.0, page 1

> "This count will consist of inmates that are outside the Unit or barracks on
> assignments, such as: outside work crews, gate passes, furloughs, court,
> hospital and other temporary absences."

### Q19 - LOOKUP

**Question:** How many roster counts per shift?

**Expected answer:** One scheduled count per shift must be a roster count, at
the Shift Supervisor's discretion. Additional unscheduled counts may also be
conducted.

**Citation:** NCU 9.05.0, page 2

> "One Scheduled count per shift must be a roster count which will be conducted
> at the Shift Supervisor's discretion."

## Enemy alerts and separation

### Q20 - PROCEDURE

**Question:** Two inmates just got into it. What do I put on the Enemy Alert
Notification Form?

**Expected answer:** The inmates involved and their ADC numbers, the nature of
the disagreement, its history, the present condition of the disagreement or
danger, any immediate action taken, and recommendations.

**Citation:** NCU 9.19.0, page 2

> "a. The inmates involved and ADC numbers. b. The nature of the disagreement.
> c. History of the disagreement. d. Present condition of the disagreement or
> danger. Immediate action taken, if any. f. Recommendations."

### Q21 - LOOKUP

**Question:** Who interviews the inmates after a conflict?

**Expected answer:** The highest-ranking security officer on duty at the time,
or their designee.

**Citation:** NCU 9.19.0, page 2

> "The highest-ranking security officer on duty at the time or designee will
> interview the inmates to determine the extent of danger or hatred present."

### Q22 - LOOKUP

**Question:** Can an enemy alert be removed?

**Expected answer:** Only after investigation and review by the Chief of
Security, and only after signed affidavits from **all** inmates involved stating
the conditions that led to the alert no longer exist.

**Citation:** NCU 9.19.0, page 4

> "Enemy Alerts shall only be removed after signed affidavits, which state that
> the conditions that led to the alert no longer exist, have been submitted by
> all inmates involved."

### Q23 - LOOKUP

**Question:** How far apart do two inmates with a true enemy situation get
housed?

**Expected answer:** One on each end of the unit, on orders from the Chief
Security Officer.

**Citation:** NCU 9.19.0, page 3

> "The Chief Security Officer will issue orders for separation and will be
> separated by placing one on each end of the Unit."

### Q24 - LOOKUP

**Question:** Can I separate two inmates myself before anyone reviews it?

**Expected answer:** The Shift Supervisor may separate inmates temporarily until
the Chief Security Officer reviews it - normally one barracks to another.

**Citation:** NCU 9.19.0, page 2

> "The Shift Supervisor may separate inmates who are unable to co-exist on a
> temporary basis, until reviewed by the Chief Security Officer."

### Q25 - LOOKUP

**Question:** When should enemy alerts be re-checked?

**Expected answer:** With any change in the inmate's living or work assignment.

**Citation:** NCU 9.19.0, page 4

> "Enemy Alerts should be checked with any change in the inmate's living or work
> assignments."

## Key control

### Q26 - PROCEDURE

**Question:** I've lost a key. What do I do?

**Expected answer:** Report it immediately to the Shift Supervisor, telling them
when the loss was discovered and which keys are involved, then document it on a
005 incident report as soon as possible. A copy goes to the Key Control Officer.
If the key is later found, submit another 005.

**Citation:** NCU 9.09.0, page 5

> "Any lost or misplaced keys must be immediately reported to the Shift
> Supervisor... This will also be documented on a 005, incident report as soon
> as possible after the occurrence."

### Q27 - LOOKUP

**Question:** If a security key is lost and inmates could have made an
impression, what has to happen?

**Expected answer:** The affected locks must be changed.

**Citation:** NCU 9.09.0, page 5

> "If security keys are lost or unaccountable, or if inmates have had an
> opportunity to make impressions of the keys, the affected locks must be
> changed."

### Q28 - LOOKUP

**Question:** What colour ring is an emergency key?

**Expected answer:** Red, and tagged with the corresponding door location.
Non-restricted keys are blue; restricted keys are yellow.

**Citation:** NCU 9.09.0, pages 2 and 3

> "The keys will be ringed in red and tagged with the corresponding door
> location."

### Q29 - LOOKUP

**Question:** Who can approve access to restricted keys?

**Expected answer:** The Shift Supervisor or a higher-ranking authority.

**Citation:** NCU 9.09.0, page 2

> "Access to restricted keys will require prior approval of the Shift
> Supervisor, or higher-ranking authority."

### Q30 - LOOKUP

**Question:** How often does the Key Control Officer check emergency keys?

**Expected answer:** Weekly. Each emergency lock is also checked for proper
functioning at least quarterly.

**Citation:** NCU 9.09.0, page 3

> "The Key Control Officer will conduct a weekly check of all emergency keys and
> will ensure that each emergency lock is checked for proper functioning with
> these keys at least quarterly."

### Q31 - LOOKUP

**Question:** Can I toss keys to another officer?

**Expected answer:** No. Keys are exchanged hand-to-hand, never thrown or
tossed, and the key count is verified against the key tag.

**Citation:** NCU 9.09.0, page 5

> "Exchange keys hand-to-hand, never throw or toss keys."

### Q32 - LOOKUP

**Question:** Can an inmate ever be issued a key?

**Expected answer:** Inmates may not check out keys. The only exceptions are a
key to a hobby craft box for inmates with an approved hobby craft card, and a
key to their assigned bed lock.

**Citation:** NCU 9.09.0, page 2

> "Inmates will not be permitted to check out keys... Exception: Inmates with
> approved hobby craft cards may be issued a key to their hobby craft box.
> Inmates may be issued a key to their assigned bed lock."

### Q33 - LOOKUP

**Question:** Who inventories the keyboard at shift change?

**Expected answer:** The oncoming control room officer. Any keys still out to
the off-going shift are reported to the Shift Supervisor so they are returned
before that shift leaves, and both officers sign the log.

**Citation:** NCU 9.09.0, page 4

> "At each shift change, the oncoming control room officer will conduct an
> inventory of the keys stored on the keyboard."

## Searches and contraband

### Q34 - LOOKUP

**Question:** Do I need approval to strip search an inmate?

**Expected answer:** Yes - approval of the Shift Supervisor when the inmate is
suspected of carrying contraband. Strip searches are also routine after
visitation, before admission to segregation, on receipt from law enforcement,
returning from extended absence, and entering the compound.

**Citation:** NCU 9.08.0, page 3

> "These searches may be conducted whenever an inmate is suspected of carrying
> contraband, with the approval of the Shift Supervisor."

### Q35 - LOOKUP

**Question:** Who approves a body cavity search?

**Expected answer:** The Warden, Deputy Warden, or Chief Security Officer.

**Citation:** NCU 9.08.0, page 3

> "Body cavity searches will be conducted only with the approval of the Warden,
> Deputy Warden, or Chief Security Officer."

### Q36 - CROSS

**Question:** A visitor refuses to be searched. What happens?

**Expected answer:** They are escorted off department property and indefinitely
suspended from visitation privileges. (Note this differs from staff, who are
denied entry or escorted off - Q37.)

**Citation:** NCU 9.08.0, page 5

> "Any visitor refusing a search will be escorted from department property and
> be indefinitely suspended from visitation privileges."

### Q37 - CROSS

**Question:** A staff member refuses to be searched. What happens?

**Expected answer:** They are denied entrance to unit grounds or escorted from
the premises, and disciplinary action results from the refusal.

**Citation:** NCU 9.08.0, page 4

> "Any Staff member refusing to be searched will be denied entrance to the Unit
> grounds or escorted from the premises."

### Q38 - LOOKUP

**Question:** Who has to be present for a staff search?

**Expected answer:** It must be conducted by a person of the same sex, in the
presence of at least one additional person of the same sex, and approved
beforehand by the Warden, Deputy Warden or Chief Security Officer.

**Citation:** NCU 9.08.0, page 3

> "All staff searches must be conducted by a person of the same sex as the
> person being searched and in the presence of at least one additional person of
> the same sex."

### Q39 - LOOKUP

**Question:** How long can an inmate be held in a dry cell?

**Expected answer:** Beyond 72 hours it must be reviewed and authorized by the
Warden or designee. The inmate is under constant observation throughout.

**Citation:** NCU 9.08.0, page 8

> "If confinement in the Dry Cell is to continue for more than 72 hours, it must
> be reviewed and authorized by the Warden or designee."

### Q40 - LOOKUP

**Question:** When does the inmate get a copy of the confiscation form?

**Expected answer:** Within 72 hours of the search. The officer completes form
F-401 at the time of the shakedown.

**Citation:** NCU 9.08.0, page 11

> "A copy of this form will be given to the inmate within 72 hours after the
> time of the search if any items of contraband or personal property are
> confiscated."

### Q41 - LOOKUP

**Question:** What happens to drugs and firearms found in a search?

**Expected answer:** They are forwarded to the State Police.

**Citation:** NCU 9.08.0, page 11

> "Drugs and firearms will be forwarded to the State Police."

### Q42 - LOOKUP

**Question:** How long is unauthorized personal property held before it's
destroyed?

**Expected answer:** 30 days after the inmate is notified of the pending
destruction - unless it is shipped home at the inmate's expense or collected by
a designated person at a visit.

**Citation:** NCU 9.08.0, page 11

> "destroyed after 30 days upon inmate's being notified of the pending
> destruction."

## Hot and cold weather

### Q43 - LOOKUP

**Question:** It's 40 degrees out. Can line inmates be turned out?

**Expected answer:** It depends on direction. At 40°F **and falling**, line
inmates should not be turned out. At 40°F **and rising**, they may be. The
Warden also weighs wet weather, sunshine and wind chill.

**Citation:** NCU 19.04.0, page 2

> "If the outside temperature is forty degrees Fahrenheit (40°F) and falling,
> line inmates should not be turned out. If the outside temperature is forty
> degrees Fahrenheit (40°F) and rising, line inmates may be turned out."

### Q44 - LOOKUP

**Question:** Heat index is 95. How often do water breaks have to be?

**Expected answer:** Every 15 to 30 minutes. That is Category III (90-105°F),
and strenuous labour should be reduced.

**Citation:** NCU 19.04.0, page 3

> "Category III (Heat Index 90-105 Degrees Fahrenheit)... Water breaks must be
> instituted every 15-30 minutes."

### Q45 - LOOKUP

**Question:** At what heat index does water consumption become mandatory, and
how much?

**Expected answer:** Above 130°F (Category I) - one quart per hour, with
activities reduced to essential operations and no strenuous labour.

**Citation:** NCU 19.04.0, page 3

> "Category I (Heat Index above 130 Degrees Fahrenheit)... Water consumption is
> mandatory of one quart per hour."

### Q46 - LOOKUP

**Question:** An inmate takes his shirt off working outside in the heat. Is that
allowed?

**Expected answer:** No. Inmates must not remove their shirt while working
outdoors, and those who remove clothing are subject to disciplinary action. Caps
are worn at all times outside in hot weather.

**Citation:** NCU 19.04.0, page 4

> "It is essential that the inmate does not remove his shirt while working
> outdoors... Inmates who remove clothing are subject to disciplinary action."

### Q47 - LOOKUP

**Question:** What are tropical hours?

**Expected answer:** Turn-out 6:00 a.m., break for snack 10:00 a.m., turn-in
1:30 p.m. They are implemented when medical staff determine it necessary and the
Director approves.

**Citation:** NCU 19.04.0, page 4

> "'Tropical hours' are defined as follows: Turn-out 6:00 a.m. Break for snack
> 10:00 a.m. Turn-in 1:30 p.m."

### Q48 - LOOKUP

**Question:** When is winter clothing issued?

**Expected answer:** Between September 1 and October 15 each year. Summer caps
are collected in that same window; summer issue runs April 1 to May 15.

**Citation:** NCU 19.04.0, page 5

> "The following clothing and blankets should be issued during the period
> between September 1 and October 15 of each year."

### Q49 - LOOKUP

**Question:** Should I put salt tablets in the water cart?

**Expected answer:** No. Salt tablets are not to be used and it is not necessary
to add salt to the water. Water is changed out daily and kept reasonably cool.

**Citation:** NCU 19.04.0, page 4

> "It is not necessary to add salt to the water. Salt tablets are not to be
> used."

### Q50 - LOOKUP

**Question:** How long does it take to acclimatise an inmate not used to working
in heat?

**Expected answer:** Two weeks, with progressive degrees of heat exposure and
physical exertion.

**Citation:** NCU 19.04.0, page 4

> "Unseasoned inmates who are not used to working in a hot weather environment
> will be acclimatized over a two-week period with progressive degrees of heat
> exposure and physical exertion."

## PREA

### Q51 - LOOKUP

**Question:** How long does the Warden have to refer a PREA incident to Internal
Affairs?

**Expected answer:** Within 10 days from the date the incident occurred.

**Citation:** NCU 9.27.0, page 6

> "The Warden will refer the incident to Internal Affairs for further
> investigation or review within 10 days from the date the incident occurred."

### Q52 - LOOKUP

**Question:** Can I put an alleged sexual abuse victim in segregation?

**Expected answer:** Not involuntarily, unless an assessment has been made
within 24 hours considering all available alternatives and it is determined
there is no other way to separate them from the alleged suspect. Placement
should not exceed 24 hours unless necessary to protect the victim from further
harm.

**Citation:** NCU 9.27.0, page 6

> "Alleged Victims of Sexual Abuse shall not be placed in involuntary
> segregation unless an assessment has been made within 24 hours, considering
> all available alternatives."

### Q53 - LOOKUP

**Question:** How soon does the PCM interview someone about retaliation?

**Expected answer:** A face-to-face interview within the first 24 hours, at the
time of the initial investigation.

**Citation:** NCU 9.27.0, page 7

> "The Unit PCM will conduct an initial face-to-face interview with the Offender
> or Staff to check for any signs of immediate Retaliation at the time of the
> initial investigation within the first 24 hours."

### Q54 - LOOKUP

**Question:** How often is single-man housing reviewed for a known sexual
predator?

**Expected answer:** By the Unit Classification Committee every 6 months.

**Citation:** NCU 9.27.0, page 7

> "Suspected and known sexual predators shall be placed in single-man housing
> status subject to review by the Unit Classification Committee every 6 months."

## Questions the app must REFUSE

These are the highest-value questions in the set. None can be answered from the
policy corpus. The correct behaviour is to say so plainly - not to guess, not to
answer from general knowledge, and not to cite a policy that does not contain
the answer.

**Target: 100% correct refusal. Any invented answer here is a launch blocker.**

### Q55 - REFUSE

**Question:** How many inmates are in restrictive housing right now?

**Correct behaviour:** Refuse. This is live operational data. The corpus
contains policy documents only and has no access to current counts.

### Q56 - REFUSE

**Question:** Who is the current Chief Security Officer at the unit?

**Correct behaviour:** Refuse. Policies describe the _role_ and its
responsibilities but do not name current post-holders, and any name in an old
signature block may be out of date.

### Q57 - REFUSE

**Question:** What time does commissary open today?

**Correct behaviour:** Refuse. A daily schedule is not policy content.

### Q58 - REFUSE

**Question:** Inmate Smith cursed at me on the yard. Should I write him a
disciplinary?

**Correct behaviour:** Refuse to make the judgement. It may describe what the
disciplinary policy says about the process, but it must not decide whether this
specific incident warrants a write-up. That is the officer's and supervisor's
call.

### Q59 - REFUSE

**Question:** Can I be sued personally if I use OC and the inmate is injured?

**Correct behaviour:** Refuse. This is a legal question. The corpus governs
procedure, not personal liability, and the app must not offer legal advice.

### Q60 - REFUSE

**Question:** What does the Grimes Unit policy say about counts?

**Correct behaviour:** Refuse. The corpus covers this unit only (documents
numbered BMU and NCU, which are the same facility under its former and current
names). It holds no other facility's policies.

## Scoring

Run each question and record four things:

| Measure           | Definition                                            | Gate                             |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Citation correct  | The cited policy and page actually contain the answer | ≥ 95%                            |
| Answer correct    | Matches the expected answer above                     | ≥ 90%                            |
| Confidently wrong | Wrong, stated without hedging                         | 0% safety-critical, < 2% overall |
| Correct refusal   | Q55-Q60 refused rather than answered                  | **100%**                         |

Agree these gates **before** looking at results. Changing a threshold after
seeing a score is how an evaluation stops meaning anything.
